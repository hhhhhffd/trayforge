# Trayforge privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

Trayforge does not collect, store or send any personal data. It has no telemetry, analytics,
crash reporting, advertising or user accounts.

- **Your input stays on your computer.** Every tool runs locally. What you paste into a tool
  is processed on your device and kept in memory only while Trayforge is running.
- **Network access.** Only the HTTP Client tool connects to the network, and only to the
  address you enter, when you send a request. Nothing else in the app goes online.
- **Local files.** Settings are saved in your user profile. TOTP secrets you choose to save
  are encrypted with Windows DPAPI for your Windows account.
- **Files you open.** Files you pick or drop into a tool are read locally and never uploaded.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/hhhhhffd/trayforge/issues.
